# Simple Kit Registrarion

This plugin aims to provide a simplified way to register applications on your website. With it, you can create forms that can be filled with information about the applications and also receive files, such as PDFs. The plugin also provides a tool to forward applications to independent reviewers, who do not need access to your website's editing panel.

## Highlights

- Create forms to receive applications.
- Customize with various types of information, including file uploads.
- Allow evaluation of responses even by selected individuals outside your team, with password access.
- Maintain as many simultaneous applications as needed.

## Available fields

The forms created can contain various types of information:

- Text
- E-mail
- Password
- URL
- Number
- Phone number
- Tetx area
- Checkboxes
- Radio buttons
- Dropdown selectors
- File uploads

<img width="1920" height="913" alt="reg2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/34f5328f-8729-439c-be8e-871e973a6b06" />

Create your page or post and include your signup form using the corresponding block.

<img width="1920" height="913" alt="reg3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/17be02d7-e2ce-4be2-a0f7-ea16fc3359d1" />

Register reviewers, indicating which forms they will have access to and what their access passwords will be.

<img width="1920" height="913" alt="reg4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0e0bef4c-8faa-4bcb-a1ac-520da7042599" />

Create your access page or post for reviewers and include the corresponding block. When they access it, they will be asked for the email and password you created so they can access the entries. Reviewers can leave as many comments as they want on each entry for a more detailed evaluation. You can, through the WordPress dashboard, track entries, reviewer comments, and even leave your own impressions, all recorded with date and time for validation.

<img width="1920" height="913" alt="reg5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bf6947a1-132e-4a6c-85dd-bc59768054f6" />

And you can even export all your forms or just a selected one to keep a backup copy of all registrations with their information and files.
